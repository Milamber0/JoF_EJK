Elegance HUD - HD textures

HD artwork by Circa, based on the original Elegance HUD by DarthNormaN.
Source: https://jkhub.org/files/file/4564-elegance-hud-hd/
Original HUD: https://jkhub.org/files/file/296-elegance-hud/

The HD health, armor, ammo, and force ticks and the fast, medium, and strong
saber bars in this folder come from Circa's Elegance HUD - HD package.
The Desann, Tavion, staff, and dual bars were adapted for JoF EJK from
Circa's HD strong bar, using the colors of this client's original bars.
Circa's original package readme is included as CIRCA_ORIGINAL_README.txt.

The JKHub download permits modification and sharing with credit to the
original author. Credit is given here to both Circa and DarthNormaN.
