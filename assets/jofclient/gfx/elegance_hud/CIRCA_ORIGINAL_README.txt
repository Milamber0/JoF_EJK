-!|!-  DESCRIPTION  -!|!-
-=======================-

The Elegance HUD created by DarthNormaN is beloved by the community for it's simplicity and...well...elegance. However it was made at a time before higher resolutions were common, so nowadays it's quite blurry and pixelated on higher resolutions, especially 4k. I recreated the simple shapes to match the style as best I could while bumping up the texture resolutions be 2 or 3 times. It's still the same HUD, but crisper.
