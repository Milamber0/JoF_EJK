SJK Menu, SJK Chat and SJK HUD
==============================

SJK draws no bitmap fonts. These three TrueType fonts replace Jedi Academy's
bitmap game fonts and keep their layout: every glyph has the retail font's
advance and sits where the retail glyph sat. scripts/game_fonts.py builds them
from the inputs below, read from a player's install; none of those inputs are
in this repository.

SJKMenu.ttf (for ergoec) and SJKHud.ttf (for arialnb)
  Traced, glyph by glyph, from the ergoec and arialnb atlases of the JoF HD
  Fonts & Icons pack (JoF_HDFonts&Icons.pk3), a high-resolution redraw of the
  fonts Raven Software shipped with Jedi Academy. The pack's author is unknown
  and it states no licence; SJK includes these tracings on the assumption that
  the pack is shared freely in the community, credits it here, and will remove
  them at the author's request. The letterforms follow the retail fonts, whose
  designs belong to their respective owners.

SJKChat.ttf (for ocr_a)
  OCR-A by John Sauter, released to the public domain (Debian package
  fonts-ocr-a), set to the widths of Jedi Academy's ocr_a. Latin-1 letters and
  symbols OCR-A lacks were composed from its own glyphs for SJK. Seven glyphs
  (ß þ Þ ð § ¶ ¤) and the "WSI fonts" logo at byte 0xAC are traced from the JoF
  HD pack as above.

The "WSI fonts" logo at byte 0xAC of SJK Menu and SJK Chat is the boxed logo
the retail fonts carry there, which players use in their names.

SJK Symbols
===========

SJKSymbols.ttf is SJK's own symbol font: marks for rarities and finishes in
Unicode's Private Use Area, drawn for SJK and built by scripts/sjk_symbols.py
(docs/sjk-ui.md "Symbols"). Nothing in it is traced or taken from another font.
It is distributed with SJK under the GNU General Public License, version 2.
